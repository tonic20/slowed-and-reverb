🎧 Make a slowed and reverb mix while a song plays. This slowed reverb maker works on a music tab or local track. Ease back the tempo changer, let the last word ring out, then switch to Original and compare.

1️⃣ Try a track
1. Open a playing tab and click the toolbar icon. On a supported player, the popup connects to that tab. You can close the controls and carry on listening.
2. To work with a recording, select the file source and drop in a song. Try demo loads a short vocal excerpt if you want to hear a slowed and reverb effect before choosing your own material.
3. Want to take the whole song slower? Choose Slowed, move Speed a little at a time and listen to the end of a sung line before adding more space.

🎚 How should the voice sound?
• To slow down music without changing pitch, switch on Keep original pitch. The singer stays in the same register. It helps when you want to follow a guitar phrase or sing along in the same key.

• Turn that switch off for the classic slowed and reverb sound. Pitch falls with playback speed, like a record turning more slowly. Switch it back on if a familiar melody sits too low to sing along.

🌙 Pick a starting mood
➤ Slowed starts at 0.82× with a dark room around the voice. For a softer slowed and reverb edit, Dream goes a little slower and lets each note ring out in a hall.
➤ Tape keeps more of the attack with a short tail. Looking for a nightcore maker? Choose Nightcore for a faster tempo with a light plate sound. You can keep adjusting after a preset is selected.

🎛 Shape the reverb effect
– Start with a small amount of ambience. Increase the mix until you hear a tail between phrases. A short reflection can suit close vocals, while the five-second Dream preset gives a sparse instrumental more space.

– In a busy slowed and reverb mix, lower the slider until the next lyric comes through clearly. To add reverb to audio with long gaps between notes, try Dream and listen to the end of each phrase. Use Original for a quick comparison at any point.

🎼 Practice a difficult passage
• Use the music slow downer to bring a fast section within reach. Keep the tuning steady, follow the rhythm, then edge closer to normal tempo as your fingers learn the part.
• To change song speed for a rehearsal, set a comfortable pace and use the seek bar to return to the same line. Release Keep original pitch to hear a slowed and reverb version in a lower key.

📁 Open your own track
– The slowed and reverb maker accepts MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG and FLAC files your browser can decode. Choose a mono or stereo recording of up to ten minutes and 50 MiB.

– Your last track stays available when you reopen the popup. Remove file clears the saved copy. This makes it easy to revisit a slowed and reverb edit without choosing it again.

💾 Save what you hear
1. In file mode, preview the passage with your chosen settings. Listen for the way a final chord decays before exporting your slowed and reverb mix.
2. Choose Download WAV to render a 16-bit PCM copy. Include reverb tail lets the room fade past the original ending. Prevent clipping helps keep loud peaks inside the available range.

3. Choose where to save the result in Chrome. Your source recording stays intact. The slowed and reverb generator exports from your computer. Tab playback is for listening.

🎹 Compare two takes
• Use the same few bars for each comparison. The audio speed changer runs from 0.5× to 1.5×. On a slowed and reverb track with a sustained piano chord, listen to the silence after the last key lifts.

🔊 What if the tab will not connect?
• Start playback on the site, then open the popup again or use Connect. Some protected streams and embedded players restrict access. You can still work with a recording from your computer.
• Slowed and Reverb works on the selected tab. Disconnect when you finish. Effects can add a short delay. If a video feels out of sync, compare with Original or turn the effect off with the power button.

🔒 Where does the audio go?
– Slowed and Reverb processes samples on your device. The extension stores preferences in the browser so your choices carry across sessions. Listening and rendering need no account or audio upload.
– To hear the difference on familiar material, start with a dry recording, change one setting, then compare. A slowed and reverb version with only a little ambience often leaves more room for the words.
