Privacy

This extension processes sound on your device. It does not upload audio, collect analytics, or send browsing history, tab titles, files or settings to a server. There is no account.

When you connect a browser tab, Chrome provides that tab's audio to the extension. The extension reads the selected page's media player to control tempo and playback. Tab identifiers and title are used locally for the current session and interface. It also detects players outside the visible page structure, including detached media and accessible embedded players. Detection starts when you open the toolbar popup in Browser tab mode or explicitly connect. It does not run automatically on every website. Playback detection may remain active for up to two minutes after disconnecting. It does not transmit detected media or playback data.

Your effect preferences are stored locally in Chrome extension storage. The last valid selected file is stored in the extension's local IndexedDB so you can reopen it. Remove file deletes that saved copy. Uninstalling the extension removes its browser-managed storage. Exported WAV files remain in the destination you choose until you delete them.

The bundled demo is stored in the extension. Its attribution links open external sites only when you click them; those sites have their own privacy practices.

The optional rating prompt keeps a local count of qualifying listening sessions (capped at two), a random deduplication token and your selected star value. It does not record which music you listened to or send listening statistics anywhere. Selecting a star opens the store reviews page for four or five stars or the extension’s Google feedback form for one to three. In an unpacked build, all stars open the product feedback form. The choice dismisses the prompt. Writing and submitting a review or feedback is voluntary.

After a fresh installation, Chrome opens the quick start on slowedandreverb.online. The welcome URL includes the extension ID and, when available, its toolbar pin status. The welcome page can ask this extension in Chrome for pin status updates so it can show the next step. After removal, Chrome opens a fixed uninstall page without an identifier. Both pages can be visited directly. The uninstall page embeds a Google Form. Loading it connects to Google; submitting a response sends the text you choose to share to the developer. The form does not ask for an email address, name or file. The website host receives ordinary page requests; the extension does not include your audio or browsing history in them.

This website uses Google Analytics 4 and first-party cookies to count visits and page views. Each page view sends Google the page URL, including its query parameters, and any referrer the browser provides. On the welcome page, those parameters can include the Chrome extension ID and whether the extension is pinned to the toolbar. Audio, local files and feedback responses stay out of Analytics.

Our use of data accessed through Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Audio and playback information are used locally to provide the audio controls. They are never sold, used for advertising or used to determine creditworthiness.

For privacy questions, contact tonic20@gmail.com.

The interface font and demo are bundled. The extension does not contact a font service. Last updated November 16, 2026.
