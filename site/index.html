02 / Inside the extension

Find your own mix.

Start with a preset
Choose Slowed, Dream, Tape or Nightcore. Try Original at any point to hear what changed.
Set the tempo and room
Move from half speed to 1.5×. Keep the original pitch, or turn it off to let the voice drop with the beat. Add reverb to taste.
Take a mix with you
Open a file from your device and download the result as a stereo WAV. Both the recording and the mix stay on your computer.

03 / Liner notes

Before you press play.

Which browsers and players are supported?

Use desktop Chrome 116 or later. See the tested players above. Some protected or embedded players may not let Chrome change their sound.

Can I export the song from a browser tab?

Browser tab mode is for live listening. To export, choose Audio file, open a recording you own or have permission to use, then select Download WAV.

What files can I mix?

Try an MP3 or WAV, or another audio format Chrome can decode. Local files can be mono or stereo, up to 50 MiB and ten minutes long.

Can I choose the audio engine?

Yes. Open Settings in the extension to choose your audio engine. Rubber Band R3 is the default for high-quality pitch and tempo processing. Signalsmith uses less CPU and is useful on slower devices. The selected engine applies to browser tabs, local audio files and WAV export. Both engines offer High quality and Lightweight processing.

Do I need an account?

No. The extension processes sound on your device, with no account or audio upload. Read the privacy policy.
